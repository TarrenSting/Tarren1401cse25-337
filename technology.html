Engineering Innovations

Redefining the limits of automotive engineering

A Different Kind of Engineering

Koenigsegg designs and builds nearly every component in-house, allowing complete control over performance, efficiency, and innovation. The result is technology that often leads the automotive industry by years.

Freevalve Technology

A camless engine system that replaces traditional camshafts with fully electronic valve control.

This allows precise airflow management, improving power, efficiency, and emissions simultaneously.

Direct Drive System

A revolutionary drivetrain used in the Regera that removes the traditional gearbox entirely.

Electric motors handle low-speed torque while direct coupling delivers seamless high-speed acceleration.

Carbon Fibre Monocoque

Ultra-light, ultra-strong chassis structures designed and manufactured in-house.

These monocoques maximize rigidity while dramatically reducing overall vehicle weight.

Light Speed Transmission

A multi-clutch gearbox system designed for near-instant gear changes.

It enables continuous power delivery across all speeds, optimized for extreme performance.
