About Koenigsegg

Engineering without limits. Performance without compromise.

The Founder’s Vision

Founded in 1994 by Christian von Koenigsegg in Ängelholm, Sweden, the company began as a bold idea from a young entrepreneur determined to build the world’s greatest supercar.

With no major automotive background and limited funding, the project relied entirely on creativity, engineering brilliance, and an uncompromising vision for perfection.

Today, Koenigsegg is recognized as one of the most innovative hypercar manufacturers in the world, producing vehicles that redefine speed, efficiency, and mechanical design.

A Legacy of Innovation

Koenigsegg has consistently challenged automotive norms by introducing technologies ahead of their time. From lightweight carbon monocoque chassis systems to revolutionary powertrain designs, the brand has redefined what a hypercar can be.

Iconic models such as the CC8S, Agera RS, Regera, Jesko, and Gemera have each contributed to the company’s reputation for extreme performance and technical mastery.

The Agera RS, in particular, became a global icon after setting multiple speed records, reinforcing Koenigsegg’s position at the peak of automotive performance engineering.

Engineering Philosophy

Koenigsegg designs and manufactures nearly every component internally. This vertical integration allows total control over performance, weight, and innovation.

The brand’s Freevalve technology removes traditional camshafts, allowing precise digital control of engine breathing for unmatched efficiency and power.

The Jesko’s Light Speed Transmission delivers nearly instant gear changes, while the Regera’s Direct Drive system eliminates the need for a conventional gearbox entirely.

These breakthroughs are not incremental improvements — they are complete rethinking of automotive engineering.

Philosophy

Koenigsegg is driven by a simple principle: there should be no limits to what is possible. Every vehicle represents a fusion of art, science, and extreme performance engineering.

Rather than following industry trends, Koenigsegg creates them — often years ahead of the rest of the automotive world.
